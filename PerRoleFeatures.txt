Team (roles, invites & permissions - epic #10)
----------------------------------------------
✔ Invite a teammate by email, even before they sign up (7-day link)
✔ Accepting an invite makes the account an organisation account
✔ One team per account (a volunteer account cannot accept - use another email)
✔ Resend / revoke pending invites
✔ Five roles: Owner, Admin, Manager, Member, Viewer
    Owner    - everything, including transferring ownership
    Admin    - everything except transferring ownership
    Manager  - posts, needs, events, applicants, attendance, certificates
    Member   - drafts events, marks attendance
    Viewer   - read-only, plus donations
✔ Change a teammate's role and designation, suspend, remove
✔ Leave the team (owners transfer ownership first)
✔ Transfer ownership to an active admin (two-step, with a confirm token)
✔ Opt-in public "Our team" list on the NGO page
✔ Every rule enforced server-side (tested role x action)
